Blood Donation Eligibility

To ensure the safety of both donors and recipients, please confirm you meet the following general criteria:

  • Age: 18 - 65 years old.
  • Weight: At least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • General Health: Must be in good health and feeling well.
  • No Recent Travel: No recent travel to malaria-endemic or other high-risk zones.
  • Time Since Last Donation: Minimum 8 weeks since last whole blood donation.
  • No Major Illnesses: Free from major chronic illnesses or infections.
  • Medication: Not on certain medications that would disqualify donation.
If you are unsure about any criteria, please consult with a medical professional before donating.
